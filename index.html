The Lab

I run a 27B model on a $300 GPU — and write down what breaks.

The most interesting part of the studio isn't the cloud models everyone has. It's the local crew, and the honest field notes from making them do real work. Green ≠ correct. Divide labor by novelty, not difficulty. The receipts are here.

→ rule: always verify yourself. green ≠ correct.
01

Green ≠ correct

A successful exit and a green test suite can still hide a broken feature. I check the actual behavior as well as the tests. Verify the result, not just the report.

02

Divide by novelty, not difficulty

Work through the unfamiliar parts with a stronger model first. Then give the implementation worker a fresh in-repo example to extend, with a clear brief and a review at the end.

03

Mind the context cliff

Long tasks can lose the brief as context fills up. Keep tasks bounded, prioritize what needs reading, and leave a clear handoff before starting the next stretch.
